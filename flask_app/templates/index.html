    SPS slow extraction — there is no single pulse: beam is bled out of the ring over seconds. The trace is the ring intensity's negative derivative from SPS.BCTDC24.51454, i.e. the instantaneous extraction rate, gated to the machine's own EXTRACTION_TIME…BEAM_OUT_TIME window so the end-of-cycle internal dump is not counted as delivered flux. Only FTARGET cycles extract; other cycles in the supercycle are pauses.

    H4 line is a separate question from the spill: the SPS can extract perfectly while our line is not powered. NXCALS publishes no North Area beam-stopper flag, so OPEN/CLOSED comes from the H4 bend-magnet currents (—) — the zone scalers cannot serve, as they sit on a large ambient floor and never reach zero. An unknown state is shown as such, never as closed.
